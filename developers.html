Comprehend is the ambient documentation platform physical therapists use to turn the visit into the note. Your product knows what happened outside the room — home exercise, remote monitoring, force plates, outcomes. Comprehend knows what was said inside it. Apps connect the two.

How it works

Your app runs inside Comprehend as an iframe, next to the clinician's note. A hosted script gives it a comprehend object.

1

Load the script

On the page you want to embed. Vanilla JS; works in any framework.

<script src="https://app.comprehendpt.com/comprehend.js"></script>
2

Provide — tell Comprehend what you know

Whenever a chart is open on your side and on ours, hand over a short markdown summary. It becomes context for that patient's note, attributed to you. Include our patient id and your patient's name — that first call is also what links the two records.

// run this on our 'ready' and 'patient' events, and whenever the clinician changes charts in YOUR app
function sync() {
  const theirs = comprehend.patient;          // { id, name, dob, ref } or null
  const mine   = myApp.currentPatient;
  if (!theirs || !mine) return;
  comprehend.provide(`## Home program — last 7 days
- Adherence: 5/7 sessions
- Pain trend: 6 → 3`, { patientId: theirs.id, patientName: mine.fullName, patientRef: mine.id });
}
comprehend.on('ready', sync);
comprehend.on('patient', ({ patient }) => { if (patient?.ref) myApp.openChart(patient.ref); sync(); });
myApp.on('chartOpened', sync);
3

Subscribe — ask the visit

Declare what you want to know, in the shape you want it back. Comprehend answers from the live encounter when the clinician acts — chart open, note generated, or "Refresh apps" — and pushes the result to you. Booleans come back as booleans.

comprehend.subscribe({
  rtm_mentioned: 'boolean — was remote monitoring discussed today?',
  pain: { now: { _type: 'number', _description: 'pain reported today, 0-10, or null' } },
  hep_changes: [{
    exercise: { _description: 'exercise name as the PT said it' },
    action:   { _choices: ['added', 'removed', 'modified', 'kept'] },
  }],
}, ({ patientId, answers }) => render(patientId, answers));

Nothing to gate on

Calls made before the handshake are queued and flushed. No ready boilerplate.

Typed, nested, lists

Structures support nesting, enums (_choices), lists sized to the transcript, and boolean / number / string / string[] / number[].

Push, not poll

You never ask on a timer. We compute when the clinician acts and cache when nothing changed.

The contract

Three surfaces, two events. That's the whole API.

CallWhat it does
comprehend.provide(markdown, { patientId, patientName, patientRef })Replaces your buffer for that patient. Markdown only, ≤ 8 KB. patientId is ours (from comprehend.patient), patientName is the name of the patient open in your app, patientRef is your id — we store it and hand it back as patient.ref on every later visit.
comprehend.patient{ id, name, dob, ref } or null when no chart is open. ref is your id if this patient has been linked before.
comprehend.subscribe(structure, onAnswers, onError?)Registers a question structure; returns unsubscribe(). Pushes { patientId, answers, contextVersion, answeredAt, partial?, errors? }. Up to 10 live subscriptions, 200 leaves each.
comprehend.on('ready' | 'patient', fn)ready once per load; patient when the clinician switches charts. Both carry { patient }.

Errors you can receive

PATIENT_MISMATCH · NOT_OPENYour provide named a patient whose chart is no longer open. Dropped. A slow fetch for the previous patient can never land in the current one.
PATIENT_MISMATCH · NAME_MISMATCHYour patientName doesn't resemble the name on our chart. Buffer and link are rejected; the clinician sees both names.
BAD_STRUCTUREUnknown _type, malformed leaf, or too large (reason: 'TOO_LARGE').
NO_ENCOUNTER · TOO_MANY_SUBSCRIPTIONS · NOT_REGISTERED · TIMEOUTAs named.

Trust model

Clinician-initiated, always

Answers are computed inside the clinician's session, on their action. Never on a timer, never from your servers. Closing the tab ends access.

Identity is the origin

A clinic admin registers your app's exact origin. We accept messages only from that origin, from the frame we created. No tokens for you to manage.

Linking is a human act

Nobody fuzzy-matches names. The clinician opens the same patient in both places; your first provide is the link, and we check the names resemble each other before we accept it.

BAA before anything loads

A clinic admin cannot enable your app until they confirm their organization holds a signed BAA with you — and they're told exactly what they're sharing. Your page never loads, and never receives a patient name, before that. Have your BAA ready.

Give clinicians a button

Link to https://app.comprehendpt.com/User/apps?add=<your launch URL>. Comprehend signs them in and opens the registration form pre-filled for their admin — or tells a non-admin who to ask.

Requirements

  • Your launch URL must be https and framable by Comprehend — the web app and the Chrome extension's side panel: Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors https://app.comprehendpt.com chrome-extension://pjafhckheppfdbidlhoedddfgebmcmnc (no X-Frame-Options: DENY).
  • We append ?comprehend=1 to the launch URL so you can render an embedded layout.
  • Load the script from https://app.comprehendpt.com/comprehend.js — don't vendor a copy; it pins the host origin and evolves with the contract.
  • Handle patient being null and ref being absent. Show "Comprehend: Ann K." next to your open chart so a mismatch is visible.

Listing review checklist

  • Loads comprehend.js from app.comprehendpt.com — no vendored copy.
  • Launch URL is https, on the declared origin, framable by https://app.comprehendpt.com and chrome-extension://pjafhckheppfdbidlhoedddfgebmcmnc.
  • Calls provide with patientId, patientName and a stable patientRef; opens the linked chart when patient.ref is present; handles patient being null.
  • Shows the Comprehend patient name next to your open chart so a mismatch is visible.
  • Subscriptions are declared once, not re-subscribed on every render; structure ≤ 200 leaves.
  • You have a BAA template ready to sign with clinics and a page (docs) that tells a clinic admin how to get it.

A listing means we reviewed those points. It never stands in for a clinic's BAA with you — every clinic still confirms its own before enabling.
